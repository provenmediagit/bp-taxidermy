Meet Bob, the BP in BP Taxidermy

When you drop off your animal, you're handing it to the guy who will personally do the work.

Hi, I am Bob Peterson, the BP in BP Taxidermy! As a born-and-raised Montanan, I've spent my life hunting, exploring the outdoors, and building a deep respect for wildlife. I mounted my first animal at twelve years old and never looked back.

After completing hundreds of European mounts and finishing an intensive professional taxidermy school covering shoulder mounts, birds, and fish, I turned my lifelong passion into a full-time career. Operating out of my shop in Stevensville, I run my business on a simple promise: when you drop off your animal, you're handing it to the guy who will personally do the work.

Every trophy tells a story. I'd love to chat with you about your latest hunt and figure out exactly how we can work together to honor your animal and get you the results you're looking for.
